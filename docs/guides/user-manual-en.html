About JeevO

JeevO is a hospital and clinic management app for small and medium hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, medical stores and laboratories. It keeps every patient's visits, prescriptions, reports, admissions and bills in one place, and connects the patient, the doctor, the front desk, the medical store and the laboratory.

Three ways to use it

  • Patient app (on the phone): book appointments, see the live token, prescriptions, lab reports and bills, and pay online, for the whole family with one login.
  • WhatsApp & SMS: confirmations, reminders, "your turn is near", report-ready messages and receipts, in the patient's language and only with consent. Works even without a smartphone.
  • Hospital web app: for doctors, reception, nurses, pharmacy, laboratory and administrators, on any computer, tablet or phone. Each role sees only what it needs.

Why hospitals use it

  • English, हिंदी and मराठी on every screen; prescriptions print with Marathi or Hindi instructions.
  • Separate owners, one patient file: the hospital, medical store and laboratory can be owned separately. Each owner sees only their own bills, income and staff.
  • Simple to start: installs from a link, no app store; works on slow internet.
  • Safe records: each hospital's data is kept apart, access depends on role, and every change is in the audit log. Nothing medical or financial is deleted; corrections are added with a reason. Built for the DPDP Act 2023.
  • The doctor decides: the app does not suggest diagnoses or medicines. All clinical decisions are made by the doctor.

Getting started

Who: Everyone

Open and install the app

  • The hospital gives you a link. It opens in Chrome or Safari on any phone, tablet or computer. No app store is needed.
  • Install on Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  • Install on iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Choose your language

Pick English, हिंदी or मराठी on the first screen. You can change it any time with the language buttons at the top of the screen.

Log in

  1. Enter the mobile number the hospital registered for you.
  2. Tap Send OTP and type the 6-digit code you receive.
  3. Tap Verify & continue. The app opens the screens for your role.

New patient? Fill in name, age, gender and language, then tap Create my Patient ID.

Safety: staff are logged out automatically after 30 minutes without use, to protect patient data. Never share your login. On a shared computer, log out from your profile when you leave.
Updates: the app updates itself. If you still see an old screen, close the app completely and open it again.

Example 1: an OPD visit, start to finish

Who: Everyone: see who does what

A patient with fever books a visit, sees the doctor, buys medicines and gets a blood test. Each step shows who does it and what the patient gets.

1
Book the appointmentPatient or Receptionist
Patient: Book Appointment → doctor → date and time → confirm.
Or reception (patient phones or walks in): Appointments → Book appointment → Confirm booking.
Patient gets: booking confirmation, token number and reminders on WhatsApp / SMS.
2
Arrive at the hospitalReceptionist
Find the patient by mobile number, Patient ID or QR code. New patient: Patients → Register patient → Quick (essential details).
Patient gets: Patient ID (card or WhatsApp).
3
Check in and pay the consultation feeReceptionist
Queue & tokens → Mark arrived → Confirm check-in → take the fee (cash or UPI). No booking? Use Walk-in.
Hospital bill (BL-) and receipt. The live token appears on the patient's phone and the waiting-hall TV.
4
VitalsNurse or Receptionist
Open the patient → Record vitals: BP, pulse, temperature, weight.
5
ConsultationDoctor
My OPD → Call next (or reception taps Send in) → notes and diagnosis → prescription → tick blood tests → Order N test(s) → Finish & sign.
Patient gets: the prescription on the phone. The lab bill (LBL-) is made automatically for the tests.
6
MedicinesPharmacist
Pharmacy → To dispense → open the prescription → check allergies → Dispense & bill → take payment.
Medical store bill (PH-) and receipt; stock is reduced.
7
Blood testLab technician
Laboratory → To do → take payment on the lab bill → Register sample & generate ID → Print sample label → Mark processing → enter results → Save results.
8
Check and send the reportLab owner or authorised technician
A different person checks the results → Verify report → Publish to patient.
Patient gets: the report in the app and a WhatsApp / SMS message. The doctor sees it in the patient's record.
9
Follow-upDoctor and Patient
The doctor set a follow-up date while signing. The patient books again from the app or at the desk.
Patient gets: a follow-up reminder.
Three receipts, three owners. The consultation fee goes to the hospital (BL-), medicines to the medical store (PH-) and tests to the laboratory (LBL-). ECG, echo or X-ray ordered by the doctor are hospital tests, done in ECG / Echo / X-ray and billed by the hospital.

Example 2: admission, treatment and discharge

Who: Everyone: see who does what

A patient is admitted for 2 days, treated in the ward, discharged and billed. Each step shows who does it.

1
Arrival and consultationReceptionist, then Doctor
Reception checks the patient in as in Example 1 (or registers an Emergency). The doctor examines the patient and decides to admit: Admit on the consultation screen.
2
Bed and admissionReceptionist (or Doctor / Administrator)
Beds & admissions → Admit patient → choose a free bed (ICU, general, semi-special or special) → attending doctor → reason and provisional diagnosis → expected discharge → advance deposit and how it was paid → insurer and policy number if cashless → save.
Admission number (IP-), deposit receipt (BL-). The bed shows as occupied on the bed board. The family gets a message.
3
Cashless approval (if insured)Receptionist or Administrator
Insurance & cashless → open the admission → record the pre-authorisation request and the approved amount.
4
Treatment, day 1 and day 2Doctor and Nurse
Doctor: ward round notes in the admission (Notes), tasks for nurses (Tasks), discharge plan.
Nurse: Nursing → Vitals every shift → Medicine each time a medicine is given → Note for observations → tick tasks Done.
5
Medicines and tests in the wardPharmacist and Lab technician
Medicines: Pharmacy → Counter sale against the patient's name (medical store bill PH-).
Tests: Laboratory → Create order with Referred by Dr. → same steps as Example 1 (lab bill LBL-).
The ward team sees the results in the patient's record.
6
Discharge summaryDoctor
Admission → Discharge summary: final diagnosis, course in hospital, procedures, medicines at discharge, advice, follow-up date, condition at discharge → Save summary.
7
Final hospital billReceptionist
Admission → Bill room charges (N days). Room and nursing charges are calculated from the ward rate, and the advance deposit is deducted automatically. Collect the balance (or choose Insurance (cashless)).
Final hospital bill and receipt. Any deposit not used is shown as "Advance deposit not yet adjusted".
8
DischargeDoctor
Admission → Discharge. The app shows any pending balance before confirming.
The bed becomes free. Patient gets the discharge summary in the app (Records → History) and a WhatsApp / SMS message.
9
After dischargeAdministrator, Pharmacist, Lab
Unused deposit: the administrator opens the deposit bill → Refund with reason. Cashless: update the claim status. Medicine and lab bills are settled at the medical store and the lab.
Bill the room charges before pressing Discharge: the room-charge button is available only while the patient is admitted. You can also bill room charges during the stay (for example every 2–3 days); each bill covers only the days not billed before.

Patient

Who: Patients and their families

The home screen shows your next appointment, your live token and quick buttons for everything below.

Book an appointment

  1. Tap Book Appointment (or Find a Doctor).
  2. Choose the doctor, then the date and time. Add a reason if you like.
  3. Confirm. You get a confirmation and reminders on WhatsApp or SMS.

For a family member: choose their name in the family switch at the top before booking.

On the day

  • The home screen shows your token number and the expected waiting time. You get a message when your turn is near.
  • To change plans: Appointments → open the booking → Reschedule or Cancel.

Your health records

  • Records → Prescriptions, Reports, History (visits, hospital stays, referral letters) and Vaccines.
  • Lab reports appear only after the laboratory has checked and verified them.
  • Show your Patient QR code at the reception (Profile → Show QR) for quick check-in.

Bills

Bills → open a bill → pay by UPI, card or net banking → View receipt.

Family and settings

  • Profile → My family → Add family member. One login covers the whole family.
  • Profile → choose your message language and whether you agree to WhatsApp / SMS messages.
  • After a visit, tap Rate your visit to give feedback or raise a complaint.

Receptionist

Who: Front desk

Register a patient

  1. Patients → Register patient. Choose Quick (essential details) for a fast entry or Full registration.
  2. Enter mobile, name, age and gender. If the app finds a similar patient it asks Is this an existing patient? Pick the existing one if it is the same person; otherwise tap No – register as a new patient.
  3. The Patient ID is created. Give the Patient card or tap Send Patient ID on WhatsApp. Tick the patient's consent for WhatsApp / SMS.

Appointments

Appointments → Book appointment → patient, doctor, date and time → Confirm booking. Open any booking to Reschedule, Cancel or Send reminder.

Queue and tokens

  1. Patient arrives: Queue & tokens → Mark arrived → Confirm check-in. The consultation fee is billed here.
  2. No appointment? Tap Walk-in to give the next token.
  3. When the doctor is ready: Send in. Patient did not come: No-show.
  4. TV display shows the token board on the waiting-hall TV.
Emergency patients always appear at the top of the queue.

Emergency

Emergency → Existing patient or New / unknown patient → main problem → Register emergency now. Never delay treatment for paperwork; complete the details later.

Admissions, vitals, billing

  • Admit: Beds & admissions → Admit patient → bed, doctor, reason, advance deposit, insurer (if cashless). Transfer bed moves a patient; Bill room charges bills the stay.
  • Vitals: open the patient → Record vitals.
  • Billing: see the Billing section and the one-page billing guide.
  • Insurance & cashless: track pre-authorisation and claim status for insured patients.
  • WhatsApp & SMS: messages to patients are sent only if the patient agreed.

Doctor

Who: Doctors

See patients

  1. My OPD lists today's patients in token order. Tap Call next · Token N, or open a patient and tap Start consultation.
  2. The screen shows earlier visits, prescriptions and reports, and today's Vital signs.
  3. Write Clinical notes: complaints, examination, diagnosis.
  4. Prescription: type a medicine (brand or generic), then dose, frequency, duration and quantity. The app warns about the patient's allergies. Repeat copies an earlier prescription. Choose Print instructions also in Marathi or Hindi.
  5. Diagnostic tests: tick the tests and tap Order N test(s). Pathology goes to the laboratory; ECG, echo and X-ray stay with the hospital.
  6. Set a follow-up date if needed. Save draft keeps your work; Finish & sign completes the visit. The prescription reaches the patient's phone and the medical store.
A signed prescription cannot be edited. Tap Correct prescription, make the change and sign the corrected one. The old one stays in the record, marked as replaced.

Refer, admit, discharge

  • Refer → doctor, speciality, place, reason → Create referral letter → print or share.
  • Admit sends the patient to Beds & admissions. For admitted patients: notes, Discharge summary → Discharge.
  • ECG / Echo / X-ray: enter and verify the hospital's own investigation reports.

Your timings and content

  • My account → Availability: working days, consultation hours, appointment length, maximum per day → Save availability.
  • Health content: review and publish health articles for patients. Only a doctor can publish.
  • A doctor with administrator rights also sees the administrator menus (see Hospital administrator).

Nurse

Who: Nursing staff

Ward work

  1. Nursing shows admitted patients and the task list, by ward.
  2. For each patient: Vitals to record readings, Medicine to record a medicine given (dose and route), Note for a nursing note or observation.
  3. Tick a task Done when it is finished. Overdue tasks are highlighted.

Also

  • Record vitals for OPD patients: open the patient → Record vitals.
  • Register an emergency from Emergency.
  • Work on ECG, echo and X-ray orders in ECG / Echo / X-ray.
Saved vitals, medicines and notes cannot be changed or deleted. If something was wrong, add a new note that explains the correction.

Pharmacist

Who: Medical store staff

Dispense a prescription

  1. Pharmacy → To dispense lists prescriptions signed by doctors.
  2. Open one, check the patient's allergies shown on screen, then tap Dispense & bill.
  3. The app picks the batch with the earliest expiry, reduces stock and makes the medical store bill.

Counter sale (without prescription)

Counter sale → search the medicine or Scan the barcode → quantity → Complete sale.

Stock and expiry

  • Add medicine: brand, generic name, strength, form, MRP and minimum stock level.
  • Stock in: batch number, expiry month, quantity and purchase price for new stock.
  • Write off expired or damaged stock; Record return when a patient returns medicine.
  • The dashboard shows low-stock and expiring batches. Export gives the stock list for Excel.
You see only the medical store's bills. Refunds and cancellations are done by the store owner.
9

Lab technician

Who: Laboratory staff

From order to report

  1. Laboratory → To do. Doctors' test orders arrive automatically. For an outside patient, tap Create order and fill Referred by Dr.
  2. Register sample & generate ID → Print sample label and stick it on the sample.
  3. Mark processing when the test is running.
  4. Enter results. Normal ranges are shown and abnormal values are flagged. Add remarks or attach a file (Add file), then Save results.
  5. A different authorised person checks and taps Verify report. You cannot verify results you entered yourself.
  6. Publish to patient. The patient sees the report in the app and gets a message.
Mistake after verification? An authorised person taps Reopen for correction and gives a reason. Correct the result, then it is verified again. Every change is kept in the audit log.

You see only laboratory bills. Refunds and cancellations are done by the lab owner.

Billing

Who: Reception, medical store and lab staff; owners and administrators

Create a bill

  1. Billing → New bill → select the patient.
  2. Add items from the lists (consultation, procedure / service, lab test) or a Custom item. Discounts can be given only by the administrator / owner and reception.
  3. Choose the payment method and amount (leave empty for the full amount; less for part payment; or Not paid now) → Create bill.
  4. Print, PDF, Share or Send receipt on WhatsApp.

Many bills are made automatically: the consultation fee at check-in, medicines at the pharmacy, tests in the lab, and room charges for admitted patients.

Collect a balance

Billing → Outstanding → open the bill → Collect ₹… (part payment allowed).

Why bills cannot be edited, and how to correct a mistake

A bill is a money record: the patient may already hold the receipt and the day's cash must match it. So items and amounts are locked, and every correction is a new entry with a reason.

Wrong bill, not paid yetCancel bill with reason → make a new correct bill.
Wrong bill, already paidRefund the full amount → Cancel bill → new correct bill.
Charged too muchRefund only the extra amount.
Item left outMake a separate new bill for that item.
Only the administrator / owner of the unit sees Refund and Cancel bill. Staff: tell them the bill number and reason. See also the one-page billing guide.

Hospital administrator

Who: Hospital owner / administrator, and a doctor given administrator rights

The dashboard shows today's patients, hospital collection, beds and outstanding payments. You see only the hospital's money; the medical store and laboratory have their own owners.

Staff & HR

  • Add staff: name, role, mobile number (used to log in), department, registration number and, for doctors, fees. Hospital roles: administrator, doctor, receptionist, nurse.
  • To give a doctor administrator rights, edit the doctor and tick Also administrator (hospital).
  • Shifts; leave requests Approve / Reject; attendance Present / Absent; Export.
  • When someone leaves, deactivate them so they can no longer log in.

Settings

  • Hospital profile (address, phones, timings, map link); Departments; Services & pricing (Add service); ECG / echo / X-ray prices.
  • Wards (Add ward; beds are created automatically); WhatsApp & SMS; Letterhead; Privacy & data.

Money, reports and records

  • Refund and cancel hospital bills (see Billing).
  • Reports: financial, appointments and operational figures; Export for Excel.
  • Audit log: who changed what, and when.
  • Insurance & cashless: pre-authorisation and claims.
  • Announcements → New announcement for staff or patients; Feedback → Reply & resolve.
12

Medical store owner

Who: Owner of the medical store

You can do everything a pharmacist can, plus the following. You see only the medical store's bills, income, staff, reports and audit log.

  • Dashboard: today's and this month's store income, low stock and expiring batches.
  • Staff & HR: add your pharmacists, approve their leave, mark attendance.
  • Settings → Medical Store profile: store name, drug licence number, GSTIN, phone and the footer printed on bills.
  • Refunds and cancellations of medical store bills (see Billing).
  • Reports and Audit log for the medical store.
The store owner account is set up once by the platform team, not by the hospital administrator. This keeps each owner in control of their own staff and income.
13

Laboratory owner

Who: Owner of the laboratory

You can do everything a lab technician can, plus the following. You see only the laboratory's bills, income, staff, reports and audit log.

  • Verify reports entered by your technicians (Verify report); reopen a verified report for correction when needed.
  • Staff & HR: add technicians; tick Authorised to verify lab reports for those allowed to verify.
  • Settings → Lab tests & prices: Add test with price, result fields and normal ranges.
  • Settings → Laboratory profile: lab name, address, phone, email, signatories and report footer.
  • Refunds and cancellations of laboratory bills; Reports and Audit log for the laboratory.

For all staff

Who: Every staff member
  • My account (tap your name): notifications, profile, leave and attendance.
  • Attendance: Check in when you start and Check out when you leave.
  • Leave: choose type, dates and reason → Send request. Your unit's owner or administrator approves it.
  • Announcements shows notices from the management.
  • You see only the menus your role needs. If something is missing, ask your administrator or owner.

Privacy and good practice

  • Open a patient's record only when you are treating or serving that patient.
  • Messages go to patients only when they have agreed; respect their choice.
  • Nothing medical or financial is ever deleted. Corrections are added with a reason, so the record stays complete.

Problems and answers

Who: Everyone
I see an old version of the appClose the app completely and open it again. Still old? Clear the site data in the browser and open the link again.
The OTP did not arriveCheck the number is the one the hospital registered. Wait a minute and tap Send OTP again.
The same patient appears twiceWhen registering, choose the existing patient when the app asks Is this an existing patient?
A bill is wrongSee Billing: refund and/or cancel, then make a new bill. Ask your administrator / owner.
A prescription is wrongThe doctor uses Correct prescription and signs the corrected one.
A lab report is wrongAn authorised person uses Reopen for correction; the result is corrected and verified again.
I cannot see a menu or another unit's billsEach role sees only what it needs, and each unit sees only its own money. Ask your administrator / owner.
I was logged outStaff are logged out after 30 minutes without use. Log in again with your mobile number.
